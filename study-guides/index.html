Calculus II Study Guides

Practice problems for each exam, with a drop-down solution for every problem.

How to use these guides

  • Work each problem on paper first, then open its solution to check your answer.
  • Mark problems as done as you go. Your progress is saved in this browser only, so it will not follow you to another device.
  • Use Show all solutions at the top of a guide to review an answer key quickly, or print a guide from your browser to get the problems on paper.

On the exams

The problems here show the kinds of questions to expect, not the exact questions. Be ready to respond in flexible, thoughtful, and creative ways to problems that may look unfamiliar at first.
